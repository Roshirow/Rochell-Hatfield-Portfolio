Email Marketing & Campaign Creative

Email Marketing & Seasonal Campaigns

Promotional email strategy, copywriting, design, and campaign creative developed for Java House Coffee Roasters to support seasonal products, merchandise, ecommerce, hiring, mobile ordering, and in-store traffic.

Client Java House Coffee Roasters
Timeline 2023–2024
Role Digital Marketing Director

01

Overview

Email was one component of a broader multi-channel marketing strategy for Java House Coffee Roasters. I developed promotional emails that brought together campaign messaging, product merchandising, seasonal creative, calls to action, and brand-consistent design.

Campaigns supported both digital and physical customer experiences, promoting limited-time offers, seasonal beverages, retail merchandise, ecommerce, hiring, and mobile ordering while maintaining a consistent visual identity across customer touchpoints.

02

My Role

Email Strategy

Planned promotional messaging around seasonal campaigns, customer priorities, product launches, and limited-time offers.

Copywriting

Developed headlines, promotional messaging, supporting copy, and calls to action designed for fast scanning and clear customer action.

Graphic Design

Created layouts and campaign graphics that maintained brand consistency while giving individual promotions their own visual hierarchy.

Merchandising

Integrated seasonal beverages, retail merchandise, ecommerce offers, gift products, and other customer priorities within coordinated campaigns.

Campaign Coordination

Connected email promotions with in-store messaging, social media, ecommerce, mobile ordering, and seasonal marketing initiatives.

Performance Analysis

Used customer and sales performance data across marketing initiatives to inform future promotional and merchandise decisions.

Beyond the Email

The goal was not simply to design an attractive email. Each campaign needed to organize multiple business priorities into a communication that customers could quickly understand and act on.

My broader role allowed me to connect email creative with merchandise, ecommerce, social content, websites, paid media, in-store promotions, and customer behavior—helping maintain consistency across the complete customer experience.
